About us

Built to Sustain … Trusted to Operate … Ready to Scale

3Lines Advanced Technologies Group was established in 2019 in alignment with Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030, with the objective of localizing military and civilian services within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The company has evolved from a spare parts provider into an integrated partner in supply chain and technical services, delivering comprehensive solutions encompassing procurement, logistics, maintenance, repair, and manufacturing.

Today, the Group focuses on supporting operational readiness, enhancing sustainability, and executing projects across the defense and advanced technology sectors, both locally and internationally — delivered by a professional Saudi team with over 30 years of combined experience.

The name 3Lines represents an integrated operational model built on three core pillars, enabling us to execute advanced defense projects, operate C4I repair facilities, and deliver integrated logistical and technical solutions that support readiness and sustainability, in alignment with localization goals and Saudi Vision 2030.

Procurement

End-to-end sourcing, secure warehousing, inventory management and distribution for military and civilian platforms.

Maintenance

Sustainment of radar, surveillance, C4I, ground support and defense systems through in-country capabilities.

Repair & Manufacturing

Localized diagnostic and repair workshops, plus reverse engineering and manufacturing of components for advanced systems.

Strategic Partnerships with Universities

Driven by the belief that universities are the core of innovation and hubs for research and development, 3Lines has established strategic partnerships with national universities to contribute to localizing over 50% of military spending by 2030, through knowledge transfer and the development of national capabilities.

King Saud University

Research and development in radar systems, and a Center of Excellence specializing in C4I systems.

Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University

Autonomous systems and simulation technologies, supporting advanced training solutions and national capacity building.

Prince Sattam bin Abdulaziz University

Research, development and manufacturing in jamming and electronic warfare systems, localizing sensitive technologies.

Qassim University

Research and development in drone technologies and advanced applications in unmanned systems.

NARS Academy

The National Academy for Radar and Space Communications (NARS) is a non-profit training institution dedicated to qualifying national talent in advanced technology fields through specialized, hands-on programs aligned with labor market needs and Saudi Vision 2030. The academy focuses on radar systems, communications, fiber optics, data centres, navigation systems and electronic protection technologies — five core specializations, with more than 500 trainees.
